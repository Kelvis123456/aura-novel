# humanizer
## When to use
Use when a passage reads as machine-written: inflated, promotional, formulaic, voiceless, or full of stock phrases. It removes those tells while keeping the meaning and the author's voice.

## Core rules
- Rewrite, do not delete. Cover everything the original covers, preserve the meaning, and match the intended register. If there is a writing sample, copy its sentence length, word level, punctuation habits, and transitions instead of upgrading them.
- Cut inflated significance: "stands as," "a testament to," "pivotal moment," "underscores," "reflects broader," "evolving landscape," "indelible mark." State the plain fact.
- Cut promotional language: "nestled," "vibrant," "rich," "breathtaking," "stunning," "boasts," "renowned." Use neutral, specific description.
- Cut superficial "-ing" tails (highlighting, ensuring, reflecting, fostering, showcasing) that add fake depth.
- Replace vague attributions ("experts argue," "observers have cited") with a specific source or remove them.
- Cut formulaic challenges-and-outlook endings and generic upbeat conclusions ("the future looks bright"). End on a concrete fact.
- Avoid AI vocabulary: delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, intricate, interplay, landscape (abstract), underscore, showcase, vibrant, enduring, foster, garner, additionally.
- Use plain "is/are/has" instead of "serves as," "features," "boasts," "represents."
- Remove negative parallelism ("not just X, it is Y," "not only... but") and clipped tailing negations ("no guessing").
- Break the rule of three when it is forced. Fix false ranges ("from X to Y" with no real scale) and elegant variation (cycling synonyms for one referent).
- Fix hidden actors and subjectless fragments when active voice is clearer.
- Hard rule: the final text contains no em dashes or en dashes, and no spaced double hyphens used the same way. Replace with a period, comma, colon, parentheses, or a restructured sentence. Scan before returning.
- Remove mechanical boldface, inline-header bullet lists, title-case headings, emojis, and curly quotes (use straight quotes).
- Remove chatbot artifacts ("I hope this helps," "Great question!"), signposting ("Let's dive in," "here is what you need to know"), fake-candid openers ("Honestly?," "Here is the thing"), and authority tropes ("the real question is," "at its core").
- Cut filler ("in order to" to "to," "due to the fact that" to "because") and excess hedging. Do not fill gaps with invented guesses: say what is unknown or cut it.
- Avoid manufactured punchlines and runs of short dramatic fragments, and aphorism formulas ("X is the language of Y"). One short emphatic sentence is fine.
- Add a human pulse only when the genre calls for it: vary rhythm, allow opinion, mixed feelings, specific unusual detail, asides, and a little mess. Do not flatten legitimate formal vocabulary, and do not rewrite phrases inside quotations or titles.
- Look for clusters of tells, not single ones. Preserve real human signs: hard-to-fabricate specifics, unresolved tension, mixed sentence lengths, genuine self-corrections.

## Diagnose
- Which sentences announce importance instead of stating a fact?
- Where is the language promotional rather than neutral?
- Which phrases appear in several places (stock words, threes, "not X but Y")?
- Is every sentence the same length and shape?
- Are there vague authorities, filler, or hedging?
- Does it open or close with ceremony (a signpost, a generic upbeat ending)?
- Are there em dashes, emojis, curly quotes, or decorative bold?
- Is there any specific, surprising detail, or only safe generalities?
- After cleaning, is it still voiceless?

## How to apply to a draft
Reply in the same language as the passage (for Spanish, also cut filler such as "cabe destacar," "en el mundo actual," "desempeña un papel fundamental," and "no solo X, sino también Y"). First give a short diagnosis listing the main tells found, with quoted examples. Then write a draft rewrite, ask yourself what still makes it obviously machine-written and answer in a few bullets, and finish with a final rewrite that fixes those, contains no em or en dashes, and keeps the original length of coverage and the author's voice. Return the diagnosis, the remaining-tells bullets, and the final rewrite; omit the draft unless asked.
